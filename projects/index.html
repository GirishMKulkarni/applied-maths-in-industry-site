Projects

The course has four modules, and each module has its own projects. In a project you get a real situation, real data and a few questions from the people who need the answers. Nobody tells you which step comes next. You choose the tools from the module, use them, and then explain the answer in plain words.

AI/ML

Sessions 7 to 13B. This is the big project of the course. You get six briefs and each team picks one. It runs for about eight weeks, alongside the other modules. You present it in Session 29, show it working in Session 30, and it is marked out of 100.

P1 The Delivery Promise An online marketplace · E-commerce and logistics P2 Where the Marketing Budget Goes An online gift wholesaler · Retail and customer analytics P3 The Lot That Fails Final Test A semiconductor fabrication plant · Manufacturing and process control P4 Tomorrow's Air A city administration · Environment and public health P5 The Plant's Electricity Bill A steel plant · Heavy industry and energy P6 The Number on the Screen A sports broadcaster · Sports analytics and live media

Financial Maths

Sessions 14 to 19. Three small projects, each team picks one. Prices and returns, forecasting, portfolios, VaR, options and backtesting. About two weeks of work, marked out of 50.

F1 The Retirement Money A retired school teacher · Personal finance and portfolios F2 Can a Computer Beat the Market? A small stock-broking office · Trading and forecasting F3 Insurance for Your Shares A parent who must pay college fees next month · Options and risk
